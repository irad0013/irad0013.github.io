About Me

Hi, my name is Guerin Tresor Iradukunda, 22 years old, the 1st born in a family of four children, born and raised in a small country called Burundi. I am passionate about music, photography, and taking videos for fun just to keep memories. I graduated from high school in june,2022. After few months, i started my university studies in the neighbouring country called Kenya, but few months after, i got admitted at Algonquin College.

I moved here in Canada june,2023 to start my studies. Starting a new life in another country far from home it was not easy. It came with a lot of responsibilities, challenges, and other life circumstances that was new to me and was not ready to face them in my young age. But i believe that nothing happens out of mistake, so i had an assignment to get used to it and be more grateful to the opportunity i was given to study abroad.

I am also a passionate web developer and i hope by the end of this course i will be able to create more engaging and user-friendly websites and i believe that this program IMD is more alligned with what i want to pursue in my future career.

Apart from my school activities, i also like to travel and explore new places, meet new people and learn about different cultures. I believe that traveling is a great way to broaden one's perspective and gain new experiences.
